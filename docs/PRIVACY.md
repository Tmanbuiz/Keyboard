# WianTribe Keyboard privacy policy

*Last updated: October 15, 2026. Publisher: WianTribe Inc. Contact: atoyejet@gmail.com. Published at https://wiantribe.com/keyboard/privacy*

WianTribe Keyboard is designed so that what you type stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what the keyboard and its companion app do with your information.

## What we collect

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK. Basic typing stays on your device. Optional online AI uses a WianTribe account (Sign in with Apple). We do not sell your data.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Typing.** Keystrokes, suggestions, autocorrect, swipe typing and emoji search are processed on your device. The keyboard does not keep a log of what you type.
- **Learned words (optional, off by default).** If you turn on "Learn my words", the keyboard stores individual words you type and which word tends to follow which, so it can suggest them. It never stores sentences. You can clear this at any time.
- **Clipboard history (optional, off by default).** If you turn it on, the keyboard keeps up to 40 recent texts you copy while it is open, for 1, 7 or 30 days, plus anything you pin. Copies marked as passwords by password managers are skipped. You can clear it at any time.
- **Settings, recent emoji and pinned text** are stored on your device.
- **Handwriting** is read by Apple's on-device text recognition.
- **Translation and rewriting on iOS 26** use Apple's on-device models. Apple states that on-device translation content is not sent to Apple.

Clearing the app's data, removing the keyboard or deleting the app removes this information. Your device backup may include it, as it does for other apps.

## When information can leave your iPhone

Only when you choose these optional features:

- **WianTribe AI.** Sign in with Apple connects the app and keyboard. We request no name or email. Our service verifies Apple identity tokens and stores an app-specific hash of your Apple identifier, hashed session credentials, account status and usage counts. Raw Apple identity tokens are not persisted. Sessions expire after 30 days; expired sessions and sign-in challenges are cleaned up daily. Only text you submit to an online writing tool is sent, over HTTPS, to our AI service to produce the result. We do not save submitted text or AI output.
- **Account deletion.** Delete AI account in Tools → AI account to remove account and session records and revoke all WianTribe sessions. App-specific pseudonymous usage records remain up to 30 days to enforce limits. You can separately remove WianTribe from Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings. Clipboard and learned words can be deleted in app settings.
- **GIFs.** GIF previews and files load from GIPHY's servers (giphy.com), which can see your device's IP address. See GIPHY's privacy policy.
- **Voice editor.** When you record in the companion app, Apple's speech recognition converts your voice to text. It runs on your device when supported; otherwise Apple may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy. The keyboard itself never uses the microphone.

## Allow Full Access

iOS asks whether to "Allow Full Access" for third-party keyboards. You can type normally without it. It is needed only for clipboard history, GIFs, copying stickers, haptics and online AI. Turning it on does not start any background uploads.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
